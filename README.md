# IELTS Academic Writing Examiner

An easy-to-use AI tool that checks your IELTS Academic essays, estimates your Band Score, and shows you how to improve.

## What It Does

- **Fixes Grammar & Vocabulary**: Shows your original text with clear corrections (`~~wrong word~~` **correct word**).
- **Gives Band Scores**: Scores your essay from 1 to 9 across all official IELTS writing criteria, explaining why you received each score.
- **Provides Model Answers**: Writes a Band 7.0+ example essay for your exact prompt so you can learn stronger vocabulary and structure.

## How to Use It

### Step 1: Set Up

Copy the instructions in `SKILL.md` into your AI assistant (such as Gemini or ChatGPT). Make sure to also attach or paste the `ielts-writing-band-descriptors.md` file into your chat.

### Step 2: Send Your Essay

Paste your task prompt and your essay into the chat using this format:

```
Task Type: [Task 1 or Task 2]

Prompt:
[Paste the essay question here]

My Essay:
[Paste your writing here]
```

### Step 3: Get Feedback

Read your custom feedback, check your estimated score, and study the Band 7+ sample answer to improve your score.
